### NOVEMBER 25, 2025
**EXA-GP Technical Details & Top 5 Papers**

#### NOVEMBER 25, 2025, 6:48 AM
**From:** Piter Garcia  
**To:** Daniel Krutz

**TOP 5 PAPERS FOR NEXT-LEVEL RESEARCH DIRECTION:**

**Paper 1: Learning Best Paths in Quantum Networks (Wang et al., INFOCOM 2025)**
- Provides BeQuP as neural baseline for routing
- Frames what neural models accomplish on path-only selection
- Useful comparison point for evolved policies with interpretable structure

**Paper 2: Multipath Inter-Domain Routing (Li et al., IEEE ToN 2025)**
- QPSEL's multi-AS design addresses scalability and topology generalization
- Useful for evaluating transferability across networks

**Paper 3: EXA-GP / Evolutionary Graph-Based GP (Desell et al.)**
- Methodological anchor for evolutionary component
- Emphasis on explicit, inspectable decision structures
- Counterpoint to neural approaches' opacity

**Paper 4: Best-of-Both-Worlds Bandits (Zimmert & Seldin, NeurIPS 2019)**
- Handles stochastic and adversarial behavior within same framework
- Relevant for reducing scenario-specific switching in hybrid approaches

**Paper 5: Equality of Opportunity in ML (Hardt et al., NeurIPS 2016)**
- Conceptual grounding for fairness as multi-objective consideration
- Useful for multi-objective fitness functions in later phases

**Strategic Vision:**
- EXA-GP could fit naturally alongside existing neural bandit work
- Not as replacement, but as interpretable alternative on equal footing
- Starting with stochastic-only benchmarking establishes clean baselines
