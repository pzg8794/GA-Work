PRIMARY RECOMMENDATION: CPursuit Algorithm
├─ Success Rate: 84% across all scenario-suite configurations
├─ Best Performance: 
│  ├─ Adaptive: 90.4% avg efficiency, 9.58% gap to oracle
│  ├─ Baseline: 95.6% avg efficiency, 4.44% gap to oracle  
│  └─ OnlineAdaptive: 88.96% avg efficiency, 11.04% gap to oracle
├─ Optimal Deployment: 1.5x capacity scale
│  └─ 9/10 wins, 90.6% average efficiency, perfect 5/5 in T-variants
└─ Weakness: Markov scenarios (87.4% avg, 12.6% gap) → fallback to CEpsilonGreedy

SECONDARY RECOMMENDATION: CEpsilonGreedy Algorithm  
├─ Markov Strength: 86.74% avg efficiency (3-4 wins per scale)
├─ Specialized Use: When Markov adversary patterns are detected
├─ Baseline Value: 93.4% efficiency at 1.0x scale (fallback option)
└─ Risk: Not recommended as primary; inconsistent across other scenarios

NOT RECOMMENDED: CEXP4, CThompsonSampling, CEpochGreedy
├─ CEXP4: 68-70% efficiency range; 0/25 scenario wins
├─ CThompsonSampling: 67-72% efficiency range; 0/25 scenario wins  
└─ CEpochGreedy: 37-39% efficiency range; 0/25 scenario wins

CAPACITY SCALE ANALYSIS:
├─ 1.0x (Resource-Constrained): CPursuit 4/5, efficiency 90.3%
│  └─ Trade-off: Lowest efficiency but minimal resource investment
├─ 1.5x (RECOMMENDED): CPursuit 9/10, efficiency 90.6%
│  └─ Optimal: Best CPursuit dominance, no over-provisioning
└─ 2.0x (Over-Provisioned): CPursuit 7/10, efficiency 90.2%
   └─ Excess capacity: CEpsilonGreedy gains ground (3/10 wins)

STATISTICAL VALIDATION:
├─ Win Pattern Stability: 70-100% CPursuit win rates per scenario
├─ Efficiency Variance: 1.5-4.5% across scales (robust, not chaotic)
├─ Oracle Gap Range: 4.44% (Baseline) to 13.26% (Markov)
└─ Consistency: 5-run suites show <1% efficiency degradation vs trends
