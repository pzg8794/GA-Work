\textbf{Key Observations:}
\begin{itemize}
\item \performance{iCEpsilonGreedy dominates pure iCMAB at 87.0\%}, with 3-run and 5-run averaging differing by only 0.4 percentage points, confirming exceptional stability across run suites.
\item \neural{GNeuralUCB achieves 89.5\% overall}, positioned as tier-1 neural choice superior to pure iCEpsilonGreedy (87.0\%), with exceptional consistency (CV = 5.9\%).
\item \legacy{EXPNeuralUCB achieves 88.6\% overall}, providing viable neural alternative with strong 3-run performance (89.9\%) but larger run-count variance (2.6pp difference).
\item \performance{iCEpsilonGreedy maintains robust consistency}, with tight CV (2.3\%) and minimal run-count divergence (0.4\%), establishing it as the most reliable pure iCMAB baseline.
\item \neural{GNeuralUCB shows exceptional convergence}, with only 0.2\% difference between 3-run and 5-run protocols, indicating highly representative and reproducible neural performance.
\item For all algorithms except EXPNeuralUCB, 3-run and 5-run averages differ by at most 0.4 percentage points, indicating robust cross-run stability for pure and Gaussian-neural methods.
\item \neural{Neural integration through GNeuralUCB demonstrates tier-1 viability}, achieving 89.5\% performance with superior consistency (5.9\% CV) compared to secondary-tier pure methods, and exceptional 3-run/5-run convergence (0.2\% diff).
\item \warning{EXPNeuralUCB caveat}: Shows 2.6\% divergence between run counts (89.9\% 3-run vs. 87.3\% 5-run), warranting careful validation across extended protocols.
\end{itemize}
