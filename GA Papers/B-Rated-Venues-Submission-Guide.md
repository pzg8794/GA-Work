## Submission Preparation Module

### Application Guidance by Venue Type

#### For Conferences (ACM/IEEE)

**Pre-Submission Checklist:**
- [ ] Verify CORE/ICORE ranking (B minimum, A preferred)
- [ ] Check page limits (typically 10-12 pages for ACM/IEEE)
- [ ] Review past accepted papers (look at previous proceedings)
- [ ] Identify overlapping PC members with your research area
- [ ] Download official LaTeX/Word templates
- [ ] Check for double-blind vs single-blind review

**Key Requirements:**
- **Abstract:** 150-250 words, standalone summary
- **Introduction:** Clear motivation, problem statement, contributions
- **Related Work:** Position vs. existing approaches, cite recent work
- **Methodology:** Reproducible, detailed experimental setup
- **Results:** Clear presentation with tables/figures, statistical significance
- **Discussion:** Limitations, threats to validity, future work

**Common Pitfalls to Avoid:**
- ❌ Over-promising in abstract/intro
- ❌ Insufficient comparison with baselines
- ❌ Missing implementation details
- ❌ Unclear threat models (for security-related work)
- ❌ Poor figure/table quality
- ❌ Missing reproducibility artifacts (code/data availability statement)

#### For Journals (IEEE/ACM Transactions)

**Key Differences from Conferences:**
- **Length:** 10-20 pages typical (more flexible)
- **Timeline:** 3-6 months review cycle
- **Revision cycles:** Expect major/minor revisions
- **Depth:** More comprehensive related work, detailed analysis
- **Novelty bar:** Higher significance threshold

**Journal-Specific Sections:**
- Extended abstract (for some journals)
- More comprehensive literature review
- Detailed theoretical analysis
- Extensive experimental validation
- Broader impact statement

#### For Special Issues

**Advantages:**
- Faster review cycle (typically)
- Thematic focus = better reviewer matching
- Guest editors often domain experts
- Lower competition than flagship conferences

**Strategy:**
- Read the special issue CFP carefully
- Cite guest editors' relevant work (naturally)
- Emphasize alignment with special issue themes
- Highlight timeliness and relevance

## Templates & Examples Repository

### LaTeX Templates (By Venue)

**ACM Venues (MobiCom, IMC, CoNEXT):**
- Official: [ACM Master Article Template](https://www.acm.org/publications/proceedings-template)
- Format: `\documentclass[sigconf]{acmart}`
- Use: `acmart` package v1.93+
- Example: Previous MobiCom proceedings on ACM DL

**IEEE Venues (ICNP, GLOBECOM, QCE):**
- Official: [IEEE Conference Template](https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates.html)
- Format: `IEEEtran` class
- Use: Two-column format, 10pt font
- Example: Previous ICNP proceedings on IEEE Xplore

**USENIX (NSDI):**
- Official: [USENIX LaTeX Style](https://www.usenix.org/conferences/author-resources/paper-templates)
- Format: `usenix2019_v3.sty`
- Use: Two-column, specific margins
- Example: Previous NSDI proceedings

### Paper Structure Examples

**1. Strong Introduction Template:**

```markdown
## Introduction

[Paragraph 1: Problem + Motivation]
Quantum networks promise [benefit], but [challenge] remains unsolved. 
Current approaches [limitation], resulting in [impact].

[Paragraph 2: Gap in Literature]
Existing work focuses on [approach A] and [approach B], but fails to 
address [critical issue]. This gap is significant because [reason].

[Paragraph 3: Our Solution]
We propose [system/algorithm name], which [key innovation]. Our approach 
differs from prior work by [differentiation].

[Paragraph 4: Contributions]
We make the following contributions:
• First contribution [with quantified improvement]
• Second contribution [with specific claim]
• Third contribution [with validation method]

[Paragraph 5: Results Preview]
Our evaluation shows [key result 1], [key result 2], demonstrating that 
[system name] achieves [performance metric] while [constraint].
```

**2. Related Work Organization:**

```markdown
## Related Work

### Quantum Path Selection
[Category 1: Cite 3-5 papers]
Smith et al. [1] proposed X, achieving Y but limited by Z.
Jones et al. [2] improved upon [1] by addressing Z, but...

### Multi-Armed Bandits for Networks  
[Category 2: Cite 3-5 papers]

### Qubit Allocation Strategies
[Category 3: Cite 3-5 papers]

### Positioning
Unlike [1,2,3], our approach [key difference]. While [4,5] consider 
[aspect], they do not address [our focus]. We build upon [6,7] by 
extending to [our contribution].
```

**3. Evaluation Section Structure:**

```markdown
## Evaluation

### Experimental Setup
- Testbed: [specifications]
- Workloads: [description]
- Baselines: [which algorithms, why chosen]
- Metrics: [what measured, why important]

### Research Questions
RQ1: [Question]?
RQ2: [Question]?
RQ3: [Question]?

### Results
[For each RQ:]
#### RQ1: [Restate question]
**Finding:** [Statement]
**Evidence:** Figure X shows...
**Analysis:** This occurs because...

### Discussion
[Broader implications, limitations, future work]
```
